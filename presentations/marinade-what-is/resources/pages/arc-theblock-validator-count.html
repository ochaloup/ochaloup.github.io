  • Daily validator count below 800, down over 65% from roughly 2,500 in early 2023.
  • Vote transactions fell from about 300,000 to 170,000 daily (40%).
  • Quote: "Validators must submit thousands of transactions daily to stay in sync with the network, and without sufficient staked SOL to generate yields exceeding these costs, running a node becomes economically unviable."
  • SFDP time-bound vote-cost support and stake matching are designed to decrease over time.
  • Non-vote transactions stable at about 100 million daily.
