# Contributors

You will find here the internal structure of the mDAO and its team members.

## Contributors

You will find here how Marinade is organised and how to join.

### Team Structure

Marinade's contributors are organised into teams rather than the two mDAO-era workgroups the DAO started with. The current teams are:

1. **Engineering:** Protocol and product engineering, DevOps, data and security. Owns the on-chain programs, the app, the APIs and the infrastructure behind them.
2. **Product:** Product direction and design across Marinade Native, Marinade Liquid, Select, Recipes, Borrow and the USDC vault.
3. **Partnerships:** Integrations, institutional and custodian relationships, technical sales and user support.
4. **BizOps:** Finance, legal, compliance, HR and operations.
5. **Marketing:** Brand, content and communications.
6. **Management:** Company direction and coordination across the teams above.

Governance sits with MNDE holders rather than with any of these teams. See MNDE Governance for how proposals are discussed, voted and executed, and for the split of powers between MNDE holders and the Marinade Council.

***

## Become a contributor

If you think you can contribute to Marinade, you can use [this form](https://tally.so/r/wkg9Zw) to reach out. If an open position fits with your skills, you'll be contacted for an interview. If there are no open positions matching your profile, we'll keep your application and may contact you in the future.

In the meantime, we strongly recommend that you read our entire documentation, join our [Discord](https://discord.gg/yTdH8YkYKg) and introduce yourself to the kitchen.
