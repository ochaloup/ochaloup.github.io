v4.3 Release Schedule

Jump to bottom
Mircea Colonescu edited this page Sep 21, 2026 · 16 revisions

This is a tentative schedule for the stabilisation and roll-out of Agave v4.3. It is for informational purposes only, and all dates are subject to change. Please wait for announcements on Discord before performing any upgrades or downgrades.

Milestone Target Date Delivery Date
Create v4.3 branch and update master branch version to v4.4 2026-08-11 2026-08-11
Testnet: Recommend nodes running Agave adopt v4.3 2026-08-17 2026-08-17
Testnet: Begin v4.3 Resume feature activation 2026-08-24 2026-08-19
Devnet: Upgrade to v4.3 2026-08-24 2026-08-25
Devnet: Begin v4.3 Resume feature activation 2026-08-26 2026-08-24
Tag Mainnet-beta Upgrade Candidate (MUC) on v4.3 branch 2026-09-04 2026-09-04
Mainnet-beta: Ask for volunteers to take 10% of stake to v4.3 2026-09-08 2026-09-08
Testnet: Begin cluster restart and downgrade/upgrade procedure 2026-09-08 2026-09-08
Mainnet-beta: Ask for volunteers to take 25% of stake to v4.3 2026-09-14 2026-09-14
Mainnet-beta: Recommend v4.3 for general adoption 2026-09-21 2026-09-21
Mainnet-beta: Begin v4.3 Resume feature activation 2026-09-28
