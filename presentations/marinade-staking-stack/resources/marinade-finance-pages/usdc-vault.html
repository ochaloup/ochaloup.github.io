The vault runs on RockawayUSDC strategy — one of the most established risk managers on Solana, automatically routing between the best USDC lending rates on Kamino. Accessible through Marinade app with no additional setup.
How it works?

1. Deposit USDC

You deposit USDC into the vault.

‍

2. Supplied to Kamino

Deposited USDC is supplied to Kamino’s on-chain lending markets through smart contracts.

‍

3. Rewards are generated

Borrowers post collateral and pay interest. Yield is generated from lending demand and market utilization. APY fluctuates.

‍

4. Strategy is actively managed

An external vault manager reallocates capital across lending pools in response to market conditions.
