Emergency council Realm

4 Levels of Governance

  • Level 1: Community sovereign – Council membership management
  • Level 2: Community + Council Veto – Realms configuration, treasuries, and administrative authorities
  • Level 3: Council Fast-Forward – Emergency council membership and council budget treasury
  • Level 4: Community Opinions – Community opinion voting (no real on-chain power)

Level 4: Community Opinions

  • Community voting (threshold 2%, 20M MNDE)
  • No council
  • 200K MNDE required to create a proposal
  • Voting time: 3 days + 1 Signing off

Level 3: Council Fast-Forward

  • No community
  • council voting (threshold 50%)
  • Voting time: 3 days + Vote-tipping

Level 2: Community + Council Veto

  • Community voting (threshold: 2%, 20M MNDE)
  • council veto (threshold 50%)
  • 200K MNDE required to create a proposal
  • Voting time: 3 days + 1 Signing off

Level 1: Community sovereign

  • Community voting (threshold: 2%, 20M MNDE)
  • No council
  • 1M MNDE required to create a proposal
  • Voting time: 3 days + 1 Signing off
